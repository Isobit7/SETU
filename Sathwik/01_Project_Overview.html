Project Overview & Authentic ISL System

Understanding the mission, linguistic realities, and architectural foundations of Project SETU — a real-time assistive communication engine for Indian Sign Language.

1. Mission & Problem Space

In India, over 18 million individuals live with significant hearing or speech impairments. Despite the rich linguistic history of Indian Sign Language (ISL), the vast majority of hearing individuals cannot understand or produce ISL gestures. This creates severe barriers in daily communication, access to public healthcare, banking, education, and employment opportunities.

Project SETU (“Bridge”) was conceived as a real-time, low-latency, edge-capable translation bridge. By converting streaming webcam video into normalized 3D skeletal landmarks, neural classification tokens, and grammatically fluent English sentences, SETU enables seamless bidirectional conversation between Deaf signers and hearing non-signers without requiring specialized sensory gloves or expensive depth cameras.

📌 Edge-First Philosophy
SETU runs its core inference pipelines on local client hardware or low-cost workstations using standard RGB webcams. It utilizes mathematical spatial normalization to remain resilient against varied room lighting, skin complexions, and camera angles.

2. Linguistic Realities of Indian Sign Language (ISL)

A widespread misconception in sign language AI research is treating Indian Sign Language as an identical dialect of American Sign Language (ASL). In reality, ISL possess profound grammatical, topological, and manual differences that render single-hand ASL classifiers completely ineffective on authentic ISL:

Linguistic Dimension American Sign Language (ASL) Indian Sign Language (ISL) — Supported by SETU
Manual Hand Configuration Primarily single-handed (one dominant hand for fingerspelling alphabet A to Z). Inherently Bimanual (Two-Handed): Letters such as B, D, P, Q, and T require coordinated spatial contact between both hands.
Grammatical Word Order Subject-Verb-Object (SVO), similar to standard English syntax. Subject-Object-Verb (SOV): Signs follow Hindi/Sanskrit grammatical order (e.g. “ME WATER DRINK”).
Non-Manual Markers (NMM) Used for facial expression and questions. Grammatical Indicators: Eyebrow elevation and mouth shapes alter the grammatical meaning of interrogative and conditional sentences.
Left-Handed Signer Symmetry Often mirrors single hand. Coordinate Mirroring: Dominant and non-dominant hand roles swap completely, requiring symmetric slot evaluation ($x \to -x$).
⚠️ Why SETU Models 126-D Dual Hands Instead of 63-D Single Hand
A 63-D single-hand vector ($21 \times 3$) cannot distinguish two-handed ISL letters like B (two flat palms touching) or D (dominant index finger meeting non-dominant index finger) from noise. SETU's primary alphabet classifier operates on a 126-D dual-hand coordinate space ($21 \times 3 \times 2$), allowing authentic bimanual letters to be classified reliably.

3. Intended Users & Project Scope

Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing Signers

Individuals who use ISL natively and need to communicate in public offices, hospitals, banks, and schools where human sign interpreters are unavailable.

Hearing Non-Signers & Clerks

Receptionists, medical personnel, and everyday citizens who can read the real-time generated English captions or hear synthetic voice speech from signers.

ISL Students & Educators

Students learning ISL who need real-time visual feedback on their hand shapes, finger curls, and spatial framing using the reverse Text-to-Sign workspace.

5. Technology Stack & Runtime Environment

Frontend Architecture
  • Framework: React 19.0.0 (Strict Mode)
  • Language: TypeScript 5.7.2
  • Build Tool: Vite 6.2.0 (ESM Hot Reloading)
  • Video Streaming: HTML5 Canvas + WebSockets
  • Audio Engine: W3C Web Speech API (Local Offline)
Backend Architecture
  • Framework: FastAPI 0.115+ (ASGI Async Loop)
  • Server: Uvicorn 0.34+ (WebSocket Support)
  • Computer Vision: MediaPipe Holistic & OpenCV
  • Neural Network Runtime: PyTorch 2.6.0 (CUDA / CPU)
  • Object Grounding: Ultralytics YOLO-World v2
  • Language Modeling: Hugging Face Transformers (Qwen2.5)
